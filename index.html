Thursday 10/22Arrival
8:07 AM

11:40 AM

Clear Canadian customs, then Canada Line to Vancouver City Centre station (about 25 min, a block from the hotel) or a cab.
~1:00 PM
Drop bags at Rosewood (check in if the room is ready).
~1:30 PM
Lunch at Fat Mao Noodles Downtown · 983 Helmcken St, ~15 min walk
Thai noodle soups: get the khao soi (creamy curry noodle soup) or the braised duck noodles, with a choice of five noodle types. Fast, casual and good value. Open Mon–Sat from 11:30 AM.
Afternoon
Back to the hotel to finish checking in, settle in and decompress from travel.
Late afternoon
Robson Square and a downtown wander · starts across the street from the hotel
Easy loop on foot, no plans needed:
  • Robson Square: the sunken plaza and terraced steps between Hornby and Howe, next to the Art Gallery. Walk south through to the glass-roofed Law Courts atrium.
  • Robson Street: head west past the shops toward Burrard and Thurlow for coffee or a browse.
  • Waterfront at sunset: cut north down Burrard or Thurlow to Jack Poole Plaza (the Olympic cauldron) and the Coal Harbour seawall. Sunset is around 6 PM, and it's a short walk from there to Nightingale or Guu for dinner.
Evening
Casual dinner, play it by ear · nothing booked
Two good options close to the hotel:
  • Nightingale · 1017 W Hastings St, ~8 min walk
    Hawksworth's casual spot: wood-fired pizza, house-made pasta, shared plates. On OpenTable if you want to grab a table that day.
  • Guu Original Thurlow · 838 Thurlow St, ~8 min walk
    Lively Japanese izakaya, small plates and drinks. Guu Toramasa (757 Seymour St, ~5 min) is even closer.

Departs 10:20 AM, back 10:40 AM (20 min flight) over downtown, Stanley Park, Lions Gate Bridge and the North Shore Mountains. Scenic Ultimate fare for Genevieve and Lucas, booking #11072896, C$418.30 total. Get there early and check the confirmation for check-in time.
