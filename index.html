Itinerary

Thursday 10/22Arrival
8:07 AM
Fly out of Santa Rosa · AS 3335 STS → PDX, lands 9:50 AM
Short 38-minute connection in Portland to AS 2225 (departs 10:28 AM). Carry-on only makes this easier.
11:40 AM
Land at YVR · AS 2225 from Portland, seats 12C/12D
Clear Canadian customs, then Canada Line to Vancouver City Centre station (about 25 min, a block from the hotel) or a cab.
Afternoon
Check in at Rosewood (drop bags if the room isn't ready yet), grab lunch nearby, settle in and decompress from travel.
Late afternoon
Vancouver Art Gallery · 750 Hornby St
Literally across the street from the hotel — easy first stop.
Evening
Casual dinner, play it by ear · nothing booked
Two good options close to the hotel:
  • Nightingale · 1017 W Hastings St, ~8 min walk
    Hawksworth's casual spot: wood-fired pizza, house-made pasta, shared plates. On OpenTable if you want to grab a table that day.
  • Guu Original Thurlow · 838 Thurlow St, ~8 min walk
    Lively Japanese izakaya, small plates and drinks. Guu Toramasa (757 Seymour St, ~5 min) is even closer.
Friday 10/23Birthday 🎂
~9:35 AM
Free Capilano shuttle from the Hyatt Regency · 655 Burrard St, Melville St entrance, ~5 min walk
No booking needed for the shuttle, and the ride is about 20–30 min. Early-fall Hyatt pickups ran 8:35–9:35 AM every 15 min, then again from 11:10 AM.
Late-October times may differ. Check the shuttle schedule the week before.
Mid-morning
Capilano Suspension Bridge Park · 3735 Capilano Rd, North Vancouver
North Shore canyon walk: the bridge, Treetops Adventure and Cliffwalk. Plan on 2–2.5 hours, then take the free shuttle back downtown. Keep your admission ticket; you need it to ride back.
~1:30 PM
Late lunch at Fat Mao Noodles · 217 E Georgia St, Chinatown
The homemade noodle spot: open kitchen, hand-cut noodles. Open daily from 11:30 AM.
Afternoon
Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden · 578 Carrall St, Chinatown, then wander Chinatown → Gastown
Quiet garden first (fall hours close earlier, so check the time), then shops, New Town Bakery for a snack and the Steam Clock on the hour. Head back to the hotel to change before dinner.
7:30 PM
Birthday dinner at Five Sails Restaurant Booked · 999 Canada Place
Table for 2 under Lucas Mays. Coal Harbour waterfront views, a favourite birthday pick in reviews.
Restaurant phone: (604) 844-2855
Saturday 10/24Seaplane & Stanley Park
Morning
Harbour Air Extended Panorama seaplane tour · Vancouver Harbour Flight Centre, 1055 Canada Place, ~10 min walk
About 35–45 minutes in the air from Coal Harbour: downtown, Stanley Park and Lions Gate Bridge, then north up Howe Sound past Horseshoe Bay and Bowen Island with the Coast Mountains behind.
Not booked yet. Book direct with Harbour Air and try promo code SOAR10 (10% off scenic tours, may have expired). Flights depend on the weather, so book a morning slot and check the cancellation policy.
Midday
Casual lunch near the West End.
Afternoon
Stanley Park Seawall (info)
Walk or rent bikes, hit the Brockton Point totem poles.
Evening
Dinner at Bao Bei Chinese Brasserie · 163 Keefer St, Chinatown
Moody, romantic small plates. Reservations via Tock.
Sunday 10/25Market & Spa
Morning
Granville Island Public Market
Aquabus over, browse the Railspur District behind the market crowds.
Midday
Lunch at the Market — pick from the food stalls.
Afternoon
Sense, A Rosewood Spa · in-hotel
Saltwater pool, treatments.
Evening
Final dinner at Hawksworth Restaurant · in the hotel
Contemporary Canadian from chef David Hawksworth. No travel after the spa, just head downstairs.
Reservations: (604) 673-7000. Sunday dinner service is roughly 4–9 PM, so confirm times when booking.
